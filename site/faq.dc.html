FAQ

General questions

The basics about our family business, our insurance, and the areas we cover across Western Sydney.

Contact

Is Johnson Property Solutions family owned?

Yes. Two generations of our family work on the tools. You will see the same faces from the first quote to the final cleanup.

Are you fully insured?

We carry full public liability insurance. We are happy to show you the certificates before any work starts.

Which areas do you service?

We work across Western Sydney including Penrith, Blacktown, Parramatta, and the Hills District. If you are just outside these areas, call us and we will see what we can do.

How do I get a free quote?

Call us or fill out the form on the Contact page. We will arrange a time to come out, look at the job, and give you a straight answer.

Do you handle small jobs?

We do. No job is too small for us. If it needs fixing, hanging, or patching, we will take care of it.

FAQ

Major works: decks, additions, bathrooms and plumbing

Answers about our major works including carpentry, decks, room additions, bathrooms, and minor plumbing.

Contact

Can you build a deck?

Yes. We build timber decks and pergolas from the ground up. We handle the framing, the boards, and the finish.

Do you manage bathroom renovations end to end?

We do. We coordinate the waterproofing, tiling, fixtures, and plumbing. You deal with one team, not five different contractors.

What is a room addition?

It is adding or converting a space in your home. This could be a new bedroom, a garage conversion, or a granny flat fit-out.

Do you do structural repairs?

We repair timber framing and structural elements. If an engineer is needed, we will tell you straight away and work with their plans.

What minor plumbing do you handle?

Leaking taps, mixer replacements, toilet repairs, and small drainage issues. For major pipework, we will recommend a licensed plumber we trust.

Pricing and quote questions

Straight talk on pricing, quotes, and what happens when we come out to see the job.

Contact

Are quotes really free?

Yes. We come out, look at the job, and give you a written quote. There is no charge and no obligation.

How do you price jobs?

We price by the job, not by the hour. You know the cost before we start. No surprises at the end.

We take bank transfer or cash. For larger jobs, we may ask for a deposit to secure materials, with the balance due on completion.

Do you charge a call-out fee?

No call-out fee for quotes. If you book a small repair, the quote is still free and the repair price is agreed before we start.

FAQ

Aftercare and maintenance

Simple advice on looking after your deck, bathroom, paintwork, and other finished jobs.

Contact

How do I maintain my deck?

Sweep it regularly and re-oil or re-stain it every year or two. We can do this for you if you prefer.

How do I clean new tiles?

Use a mild detergent and warm water. Avoid harsh chemicals. We will leave you with care instructions after the job.

What if my paint chips later?

Keep a small pot of the paint for touch-ups. If you need us to come back and patch it, we are happy to.

How often should I clear gutters?

Twice a year is a good rule. If you have large trees nearby, more often. We can add you to a regular maintenance list.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes. Many of our customers have us back each year for gutters, decks, and general repairs. It keeps the house in good shape.
